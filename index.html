Spice Isle Adventures Tours & Water Sports runs private charters from Grand Anse, on Grenada's sheltered south-west coast. Every outing is booked for one party only, so the day bends around you: a later start, a longer swim, one more bay before the sun goes.

Choose a sunset cruise, a private island tour, a snorkel over the sculpture park, dinner on board, or a party on the water. The crew bring the stories; guests say that is half of what they remember.

The highlight guests name most

A gallery under five metres of clear water

Just north of St. George's, in Molinere Bay, sculptures stand on the seabed among coral and reef fish. It opened in 2006 and is widely recognised as the first underwater sculpture park in the world.

The crew anchor close by, hand you a mask and guide you from piece to piece, telling the story of each as you go.
